Hi friends, 

(It's finally fall break! (Just one more class!))

Two week ago, I went to a lecture on Digital Minimalism[^1], and was quite inspired. ("What if you spend the time you spend on your phone being in-person with friends, calling those further way, playing sports, or taking a walk...") The next day I unsubscribed from all my extra newsletters and offloaded Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Amazon, Kickstarter, CarGurus, you name it. It's been so nice to pick up your phone only to realize there's nothing enthralling there, which, has always been the case, except the case was less clear in the NNNOOOIIISSSEEE. 

[^1]: And the "Joy Of Missing Out" (JOMO). Such a helpful phrase. 

Not saying that everyone should offload their social media. (I kept Telegram and WeChat. Plus I didn't even delete my accounts; they're still accessible via browser.[^2]) I think part of the reason why I enjoy not having them on my phone so much is because, I *really* don't like instant messaging...

[^2]: I know there's always the browser, but I'm too much of a perfectionist with user interfaces/experience to enjoy using it...

Who does, you might say. True. But I dislike it to the point where I intentionally choose to not respond to people sometimes. This, combined with other things (e.g. my personal laziness and lack of love, which I take full blame for), might have caused more disappointment and regretfulness in both others and myself than necessary. Thus, allow me to rant a bit about my communication philosophy and preferences here. Not so that you need to check yourself against the rubric every time you reach out to me, but more so that you can (either give up all false hopes in me or) hopefully be assured that though it might not seem that way from yours or even most people's perspectives, I do care about you and want to catch up, see, and be with you. 

For years my phone is by default on Do Not Disturb. If you want me right then, just call, sometimes twice to pass the Do Not Disturb filter. Don't worry if I am in something important and that your call will ruin it; I can always hang off and/or mute and will never blame you for "calling at a bad time." You didn't know, and I prefer fun things that are at-the-moment much, much more than planned ones (due my upbringing I think). If others I'm with thinks I'm a bad person for receiving a call, then I should probably re-evaluate the relationship and/or my decisions. 

If you're nearby and I get to see you on a regular basis, then I definitely will not consistently text you apart from seeing you in person. I am quite consistent with my inconsistency in this sense. For the most part I think this can go without explanations.[^3]

[^3]: This here is a real rant on something even less relevant: I might not even attend to you when you're right there, depending on the situation. I might ditch you to talk to a resident or someone who's standing somewhere looking alone and desolate. (For this point though I'm actively debating about: Outreach, or give the person I'm with undivided attention? It's much easier when both parties agree [sigh].) I could be tired too, which, I do try to give all the strength I have left for those I'm with. Or, I could just be totally drained or in a very bad state of mind, for which I apologize in advance...

If you are far away, in that I need to make intentional arrangements to see you, I still will not consistently text, or call, or video call...I will text you when I think about you, say, when I hear your (former) favorite song, see a flower we once picked together on a walk, eat a fun dish, read about something I know you'd really enjoy, etc. Something that triggers a memory. But probably no more...In addition to messaging, I also *really* don't like consistent calling and/or video calling, to the extent that I think it's almost worse than nothing, that is, if I do get to see you once or twice a year, for the following reasons: 

1. I tend to prioritize people who are physically present near me, not necessarily because we are closer in relationship, but because, they are just physically closer; they are my literal neighbors. (And, if you didn't know, I'm kinda slowly becoming a "social butterfly", albeit nontypical.) As a result / side effect, I'm often out of social energy at the end of the day, and just don't want to call, where I have to think about things to say to carry on the conversations. I just want to be with you in person, and do stuff together, or do nothing together, and let talking be a natural side effect of us being physically together.
2. Due to #1, I might end up hang up on or simply ditching 90% of your calls, planned or not. Then I get mad you and at myself for never making time and energy to answer, and, most importantly, for being mad at you whom I really appreciate. I don't the first thought that comes to mind when I think about you be the number of phone calls from you that I declined...If you're ok with only 10% of the calls coming through, I guess it's less of a problem, but it's still quite probable that disappointment will build up through the process in both parties, and that's just not necessary. 

So much for all the things I will *not* do. "Will Ina ever do anything?" You might ask. It's actually a question I get pretty often...so I'm just bad and you're not alone. 

My current vision for what I will do for you is as follows: 
- **If you think we are friends or were once friends**, no matter howsoever briefly and no matter how terribly our conversations ended or never ended, and no matter when was the last time we talked, **then you are still a friend**, and I will continue to hold you as a friend. My friendship tier goes like stranger < acquaintance < friend < close friend, ordered from don't know to know quite well. Until you request me not to hold you as a friend, at which I will respect your free choice, our "relationship status" holds. 
- **If you are a friend, then please do let me know when you're coming to my vicinity.** Heads up or not, I will *very, very happily* try my *very, very best* to drop everything I can to spend time with you and catch up with you and bring you into my life (and host you if I have the necessary conditions). 
- **If you are a friend, and you want to ask me for any favor, then go for it**, whenever, wherever. I will gladly say yes if it's within my ability to help and give you all I've got, or simply say no with no bad feelings on my end, so you don't have to feel bad if you have more to ask later. I won't get frustrated or impatient, even if you don't think you'll ever have anything to offer to me. (Trust that God as grown my patience through my upbringing and also working at the IT Helpdesk.) No questions are dumb; none is offensive unless you intend them to be. 

That's all I can think of right now. Hopefully the setting of expectations was somewhat helpful (in addition to almost definitely saddening...). But I think this is a system that could actually work with my personality (preference for spontaneity) and upbringing (ability to thrive in external chaos more than order; quality time) without introducing unnecessary burdens, disappointment, or bitterness for all of us, hopefully. If I my communication philosophy undergoes drastic changes, I might post an update on my website, which, provides a perfect segue...

"How do I know where you are? Do I not get any updates?" You might ask. I'll try to send out **email** newsletters, which you can sign up for on the homepage of this website. Newsletters will also be consistently inconsistent :D Maybe someday I'll add a real-time location tracking to the site; that could be cool. By the way, if you also have a newsletter, I would love to sign up! Also, you can always text me, or email me at hi@i7t5.com, which should always point to an active email address of mine. I can guarantee that AI automation will not enter into my personal communications and that I will read every email. Except, I am just as bad at getting back to emails as with text, so apologies and thanks for bearing with me in advance.
